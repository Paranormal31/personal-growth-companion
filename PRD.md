# Personal Growth Companion
## Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Product Vision
A mobile-first personal growth companion that helps users understand themselves, build better habits, manage their lives, and appreciate their progress.

Inspired by the motivational experience of Fabulous, with a stronger focus on personal tracking and self-appreciation.

**Core philosophy:** Every small win deserves recognition.

## 2. Core Product Structure

The app revolves around three stages:

### Stage 1 — Check In
- Interactive mood meter.
- Express current emotions.
- Optional personal notes.
- Emotion-responsive UI.
- Historical mood entries.

### Stage 2 — Grow
- Personalized daily tasks.
- Daily schedule management.
- Budget and expense tracking.
- Eating habit tracking.
- Habit formation and routines.
- AI personal companion.
- Motivational feedback after task completion.
- Celebrations and achievements.

### Stage 3 — Reflect
- Daily reflections.
- Weekly and monthly summaries.
- Mood history.
- Productivity trends.
- Financial insights.
- Eating habit patterns.
- Personal milestones.

## 3. Motivation System
Motivation is a fundamental product feature, not an afterthought.

The experience should include:
- Celebrations after completing tasks.
- Positive reinforcement.
- Self-appreciation messages.
- Progress animations.
- Streaks and milestones.
- Encouragement after setbacks.

Avoid guilt-based messaging, excessive notifications, and punishing streak systems.

## 4. AI Companion
An integrated AI assistant that:
- Helps organize daily activities.
- Suggests realistic goals.
- Understands user progress.
- Identifies behavioural patterns.
- Provides encouragement.
- Helps users reflect and improve.

The assistant should feel supportive, conversational, and non-judgmental.

## 5. Design Philosophy
- Mobile-first.
- Emotionally engaging.
- Playful and interactive.
- Minimal cognitive load.
- Beautiful animations and transitions.
- Strong visual feedback.
- Personal rather than corporate.
- Encouraging rather than demanding.

Take inspiration from Fabulous without directly copying its branding or interface.

## 6. Current Development Scope — Phase 1: Mood Meter UI

The immediate objective is to build the first interaction users experience when opening the app.

### Requirements
1. Full-screen mood experience.
2. Large expressive animated face.
3. Sliding mood interaction.
4. Mood-dependent facial expressions and background colours.
5. Optional note input.
6. Submit interaction.
7. Smooth transitions between emotional states.
8. Mobile-first responsive layout.

The visual direction should follow the supplied reference images: expressive faces, bold colours, and an extremely simple interface.

### Out of Scope for Phase 1
- Dashboard.
- Budget tracking.
- Task management.
- AI integration.
- Analytics.
- Backend persistence.

These belong to subsequent development phases.

## 7. Product Development Philosophy
Build incrementally.

Prioritize the emotional experience and usability before expanding functionality.

Every feature should contribute to one objective:

**Help users become better versions of themselves while making them appreciate who they are today.**
